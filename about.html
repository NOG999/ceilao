01 / WHO WE ARE
THE CEILAO PHILOSOPHY

ENTERTAINMENT
WITHOUT BORDERS.

At Ceilao Enterprises, we believe extraordinary entertainment should have no boundaries. Our passion is to bring the world's finest talent, spectacular productions, and unforgettable live experiences to Sri Lanka, giving local audiences the opportunity to witness international entertainment at its very best, right here in their motherland.

With a vision to redefine Sri Lanka's entertainment landscape, Ceilao Enterprises is a dynamic event management and entertainment company dedicated to connecting global talent with local audiences. From internationally acclaimed performers and live concerts to comedy, theatrical productions, and one-of-a-kind entertainment experiences, we are committed to delivering events that inspire, excite, and leave lasting impressions.

02 / WHAT DRIVES US

Our journey is driven by a simple yet powerful ambition: to bring the world stage to Sri Lanka.

Through strategic international collaborations, innovative event concepts, and a commitment to excellence, we aim to elevate the standards of live entertainment while positioning Sri Lanka as an exciting destination on the global entertainment map.
