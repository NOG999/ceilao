MATTERS.

We respect your privacy. This page explains how enquiries sent through our website may be handled.

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we ask for your name, email address, enquiry category and message. Providing your phone number is optional.

Why we collect it

We use this information to respond to your request and communicate about the events, sponsorship opportunities or services you asked about.

Form processing

Website enquiries are submitted to Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form-processing service, and routed to events@ceilao.com.au. Submitted details may be transmitted to and temporarily retained by that provider. Please avoid sending payment card numbers, passport details or other sensitive information through this form.

Questions

If you have questions about personal information you have shared, please contact Ceilao Enterprises using our enquiry form.

This information must be reviewed against the organization's actual privacy practices and applicable law before public launch.
